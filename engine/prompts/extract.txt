You are analysing user feedback about quick-commerce grocery apps in India (Blinkit, Zepto,
Instamart, BigBasket) for a study on why people buy from the same few categories and what stops
them trying new ones.

Text may be English, Hindi, or Hinglish (Hindi written in Latin script). Analyse it in whatever
language it arrives in — do not translate first, the phrasing carries the signal.

For each document return an object with these fields. Use ONLY the listed enum values.

habit_driver — why this person repeats purchases (0 or more):
  time_pressure | trust | satisficing | list_reuse | price_certainty | household_routine

barrier — what stops them buying from a new category (0 or more):
  awareness        (did not know it was sold here)
  trust_quality    (doubts freshness, authenticity, condition)
  price_risk       (unsure whether the price is fair)
  choice_overload  (too many options, no basis to choose)
  no_trigger       (saw it, no reason to act at that moment)
  size_uncertainty (cannot judge quantity or pack size)
  return_anxiety   (worried about being stuck with the wrong thing)
  channel_loyalty  (already buys that category elsewhere)

discovery_channel — how they find products (0 or more):
  search | homepage_banner | recommendation_rail | word_of_mouth | social | offline_store |
  festive_push | accidental

information_gap — what they'd need to know before trying something new (0 or more):
  price_benchmark | brand_familiarity | freshness_expiry | size_guidance | returnability |
  reviews | usage_guidance

segment_signal — life-stage or context clues (0 or more):
  pet_owner | parent_young_child | lives_alone | cooks_daily | student |
  working_professional | tier1_metro | tier2_plus

categories_mentioned — free-text product categories named, lowercase
habit_signal — 0 to 3. 0 = no routine language. 3 = explicit ("every week", "same list", "reorder")
sentiment — positive | neutral | negative | mixed
frustration — short free-text phrases, only where a frustration is actually expressed

pain_statement — ONE sentence naming the unmet need in the user's own framing. This gets clustered
  into themes, so make it specific and mechanism-bearing. Write "cannot tell how much dog food a
  month needs, so keeps buying the familiar 5kg bag offline" — not "wants better pet supplies".
  Null when the document expresses no unmet need.

evidence_quote — a VERBATIM substring of the source text, copied character for character. Do not
  paraphrase, do not fix spelling, do not trim punctuation mid-word. An automated check asserts this
  string appears in the source; anything else is recorded as a hallucination.

confidence — 0.0 to 1.0, your confidence in this extraction
insufficient_signal — true when the document is too vague to extract from. Set it and leave the
  arrays empty. Do NOT guess: an invented barrier is indistinguishable from a real one once it has
  been counted, and it will corrupt the prevalence numbers this whole study rests on.

Return ONLY a JSON array, one object per input document, in the same order, each including its
"doc_id".
