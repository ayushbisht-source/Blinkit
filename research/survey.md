# Supplementary Survey — build as a Google Form, ship tonight

**Role:** breadth, not depth. Interviews tell you *why* (mechanism); a survey tells you *how many*
(prevalence). If interviews land, this corroborates them. If interviews fall short, this is the
primary-research floor — thinner than the brief asks for, but real, and honestly labelled.

Distribute to: college/course WhatsApp groups, hostel and society groups, Instagram story with the
link, LinkedIn, r/india-adjacent city subreddits. Target 40+ responses. Realistic in 48 hours.

**Framing line to post with it:**
> 2-minute survey for my graduation project on grocery delivery apps. No email needed, completely
> anonymous.

---

## Section 1 — Screener (determines whether the response counts)

**Q1.** How many times did you order from Blinkit / Zepto / Instamart / BigBasket last month?
`0` · `1–2` · `3–5` · `6–10` · `10+`
→ *Segment = 3+*

**Q2.** How long have you been using these apps?
`< 3 months` · `3–6 months` · `6–12 months` · `1 year+`
→ *Segment = 6 months+*

**Q3.** Which of these did you buy **in the last month**? *(multi-select)*
Fruits & vegetables · Dairy & bread · Snacks & beverages · Household essentials · Personal care ·
Baby care · Pet supplies · Health & pharma · Home & kitchen · Electronics & accessories · Meat & fish
→ *Segment = selected ≤2. This question alone gives you the CER denominator picture.*

---

## Section 2 — Behaviour

**Q4.** Think about your last order. How much of it was things you've bought before?
`All of it` · `Most of it` · `About half` · `Mostly new things`

**Q5.** How do you usually build your cart? *(single)*
Search for exactly what I need · Reorder from past orders / saved list · Browse categories ·
Whatever the homepage shows me

**Q6.** When did you last buy something from a category you'd **never** bought on the app before?
`This month` · `2–3 months ago` · `Longer ago` · `Never` · `Can't remember`

---

## Section 3 — Barriers *(the section that maps to the engine)*

**Q7.** There are categories on these apps you've never bought from. What stops you? *(multi-select,
randomise order)*
- I didn't know they sold it → `awareness`
- I'm not sure the quality/freshness would be good → `trust_quality`
- I don't know if the price is fair → `price_risk`
- Too many options, I wouldn't know which to pick → `choice_overload`
- I just never think about it while ordering → `no_trigger`
- I can't tell what size/quantity I'd need → `size_uncertainty`
- Returning it would be a hassle if it's wrong → `return_anxiety`
- I already buy that somewhere else → `channel_loyalty`

**Q8.** Before trying a new category, what would you most want to know? *(pick up to 2)*
Whether the price is fair vs my usual shop · Whether brands I recognise are available · How fresh it
is / expiry · What size or quantity to buy · Whether I can return it · What other people rated it ·
Which one is right for my needs

**Q9.** *(open text, optional)* Is there something you'd happily buy on these apps but currently
don't? What's stopping you?
→ *Free text feeds the same enrichment pipeline as the public corpus. This is the single most
valuable question on the form — a handful of good answers here carry real weight.*

---

## Section 4 — Context

**Q10.** Which apply to you? *(multi-select)* — Live alone · Live with family · Have a pet · Have a
child under 5 · Cook most days · Student · Working

**Q11.** City — `Metro (Delhi/Mumbai/Bangalore/Hyderabad/Chennai/Pune)` · `Other city`

**Q12.** *(optional)* Would you be up for a 15-minute call about this? Drop your number.
→ **Leave this in even if you think interviews won't happen.** A survey that reaches 40 people
usually converts 3–5 into willing interviewees, and they're pre-screened by Q1–Q3. This is the
cheapest recruitment channel you have.

---

## Analysis

Filter to segment-qualified responses (Q1 ≥ 3, Q2 ≥ 6 months, Q3 ≤ 2 categories). Report Q7 and Q8
as frequency distributions against the engine's `barrier[]` and `information_gap[]` prevalences —
same vocabulary, so they compare directly. Where survey and corpus disagree, that disagreement is
a finding: the corpus is complaint-shaped, the survey is not.

Never quote a percentage from n<20. Report raw counts.
