# Interview Screener — Habitual Narrow Repeaters

**Recruit 8. Expect 6 to actually happen.** Interviews are the critical path — every other part of
this project can be computed overnight, this one cannot. Send these messages today.

---

## Recruitment message (WhatsApp / Instagram / college groups)

> Hey! I'm doing my graduation project on how people shop on quick-commerce apps (Blinkit / Zepto /
> Instamart). Looking for people who order at least a few times a month to do a **30-minute call** —
> I just want to hear how you actually shop, no prep needed, nothing to sell. Free whenever suits
> you this week. Interested?

Then screen with the four questions below before booking.

## Screening questions

| # | Question | Include if |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | How many times did you order from Blinkit/Zepto/Instamart last month? | **≥3** |
| S2 | How long have you been using these apps? | **≥6 months** |
| S3 | Off the top of your head, what did you buy in the last month? *(don't prompt with a category list)* | Names **≤2 broad categories** — this is the key filter |
| S4 | Do you work in product, design, marketing, or at a q-commerce company? | **Exclude if yes** |

S3 is the one that defines the segment. If someone rattles off six categories, they're an explorer —
politely thank them and move on. You want the person who says "just groceries, mostly" and then
looks a bit surprised when they realise that's all it's ever been.

## Quota

Aim for a spread across at least two of: has a pet, has a young child, lives alone, cooks daily.
These are the life-stage signals the discovery engine will flag as adjacency triggers, and having
them represented lets you check whether the trigger logic holds up against real people.

## Logistics

- 30 minutes, video call, **recorded with permission**
- Ask them to have the app open on their phone during the call — memory is unreliable, order history
  is not
- Auto-transcribe (Google Meet / Zoom / Otter). Transcripts feed the same enrichment pipeline as the
  public corpus, so don't skip this
- Anonymise on save: `research/transcripts/P01.md` … `P06.md`, no real names anywhere in the repo

## Consent line (read at the start)

> This is for a student project. I'll record it so I don't have to take notes, I'll strip your name
> from anything I write up, and nothing goes anywhere except my submission. Okay to record?
