## Analyst notes

Written immediately after receipt, before coding against the schema.

**What surprised me:** Q2 didn't work as designed. The question assumes non-purchase is a *choice* —
"where do you buy it instead?" — but P01's answer is that the category is simply irrelevant to them.
No pet, no child, therefore no purchase and no alternative channel. There is no barrier to remove
because there is no latent demand to unlock.

That is not a finding the survey could have produced. The survey's barrier list
(`awareness`, `trust_quality`, `price_risk`, `choice_overload`, `no_trigger`, `size_uncertainty`,
`return_anxiety`, `channel_loyalty`) has **no option for "this category does not apply to me."**
Every option presumes the respondent might plausibly want the thing. A respondent in P01's position
would have been forced into a barrier that misrepresents them.

**Second observation, Q4 and Q5 together:** P01's one genuine category crossover (refined oil) was
driven by *urgency*, not interest — "I needed that very urgent." Q5 then generalises it unprompted:
*"I don't shop everyday, I shop when I needed the products."* Purchase follows need; the app is
consulted after the need exists, never before.

**Q3 is the one that maps cleanly** to existing schema fields, and it names three things in order:
quality, quantity, pricing.

## Coding against `engine/schema.py`

| Field | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| `habit_driver` | — | none expressed; purchasing is need-triggered rather than routine |
| `barrier` | *(no applicable value)* | see note above — "category is irrelevant" is not in the enum |
| `discovery_channel` | `search` | goes to the app with the product already in mind |
| `information_gap` | `freshness_expiry` (quality), `size_guidance` (quantity), `price_benchmark` | Q3, verbatim order |
| `segment_signal` | — | explicitly not a pet owner, not a parent |
| `habit_signal` | 0 | "I don't shop everyday… when I needed the products" |
| `pain_statement` | Buys only when a specific need arises, so categories with no current need are never considered regardless of what the app shows. | Q4 + Q5 |

## Against the engine and survey

| Claim | P01 |
|---|---|
| Barriers block latent demand (survey's framing) | **Challenged** — for two named categories there is no latent demand to block |
| Crossover is event-triggered, not curiosity-driven (`docs/03-problem-definition.md` §5) | **Confirmed** — refined oil, bought under urgency |
| Users arrive knowing what they want | **Confirmed** — "I shop when I needed the products" |
| Information needed before trying: quality, size, price | **Confirmed** — named unprompted, in that order |

**Caveat:** n=1. P01 is a single respondent, currently in a location with only one app available,
which is itself unusual and may shape the answers. Nothing here is generalised.
